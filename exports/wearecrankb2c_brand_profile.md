# Organisation Profile: wearecrankb2c

## Organisation Info

**Brand Name:** wearecrankb2c

**Tagline:** Performance marketing driven by data. Focused on results.

**Industry:** Digital Marketing / Performance Marketing

**Overview:**
Crank is a data-driven performance marketing agency specializing in ecommerce website optimization and revenue growth. They focus on measurable outcomes rather than vanity metrics, helping D2C brands grow revenue efficiently by prioritizing ROAS, customer lifetime value, and sustainable profitability. The agency offers services including paid media optimization across Meta, Google, TikTok and Shopping platforms, creative testing and audience expansion, profit-level ROAS tracking with server-side implementation, and lifecycle marketing strategies. Their approach combines data science with creative testing, emphasizing accurate measurement through server-side tracking and profit-based attribution that accounts for product margins, shipping costs, and returns. Crank has worked with notable clients including RevitaLash, Déese Pro, iS Clinical, Samsung, Liberty London, and others, delivering results such as £125k revenue recovery and 60% year-on-year growth for Berghaus, and 300% conversion uplift for a health and beauty client.

- **Sustainable Growth**: We build retention strategies and lifecycle marketing that increases customer lifetime value for long-term success.

## Brand Guidelines

**Tone of Voice:**
Direct, data-driven, and no-nonsense. Focused on measurable results and profit rather than vanity metrics. Confident but transparent about capabilities and limitations.

**Key Words:** evidence-based, profit-focused, transparent, results-oriented, pragmatic

**Brand Dos:**
Focus on metrics that matter (ROAS, LTV, profit margins). Test systematically and iterate on winners. Implement proper server-side tracking and profit-based attribution. Measure performance after reality (accounting for returns and refunds). Prioritize actions backed by evidence. Scale spend only after proving what works.

**Brand Donts:**
Chase vanity metrics or inflated pixel data. Optimize without clean data infrastructure. Ignore product margins and returns in profitability calculations. Make guarantees about specific ROAS.
