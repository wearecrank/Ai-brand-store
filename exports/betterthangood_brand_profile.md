# Organisation Profile: betterthangood

## Organisation Info

**Brand Name:** betterthangood

**Tagline:** The digital workshop of Garrett and Iain

**Industry:** Digital Transformation, AI, Automation, Business Consulting

**Overview:**
Better Than Good is a digital workshop founded by Garrett Winder and Iain Harper that helps small businesses modernize their operations by integrating scattered apps, data, and processes. The firm specializes in digital transformation, AI strategy, and automation solutions. With combined experience spanning web design, social analytics, financial services, and artificial intelligence, Garrett and Iain help organizations eliminate operational chaos through workflow mapping, process automation, and implementation of agentic AI systems. Rather than offering generic service lists, they focus on solving specific complex problems—from rebuilding workflows before automation to transforming billing and onboarding processes. The company scales through building proprietary tools and agentic teams rather than traditional hiring, allowing them to deliver customized solutions while maintaining hands-on involvement from both founders.

## Brand Guidelines

**Tone of Voice:**
Conversational, direct, and self-aware. The brand speaks with friendly confidence, avoiding corporate jargon and pretension. They use humor and colloquialisms while maintaining expertise and credibility.

**Key Words:** pragmatic, experienced, humorous, direct, confident

**Typography:** Primary: btg-sans | Secondary: BlinkMacSystemFont, -apple-system, sans-serif | btg-sans is the primary typeface with fallbacks to system fonts. Used across all typography scales from body text (22px) to large headings (106px).

**Colour Palette:**
- Black (#000000) - Primary text and borders
- Dark Charcoal (#111111) - Body text and secondary elements
- White (#FFFFFF) - Heading text and contrast on dark backgrounds

**Brand Dos:**
Be direct about what you do and avoid corporate fluff. Share real examples of problems solved. Emphasize your lived experience and track record. Use conversational, accessible language. Focus on outcomes and client self-sufficiency. Acknowledge the complexity while explaining simply.

Focus on solving real business problems. Be direct and honest about capabilities. Use humor and personality in communication. Share actual case studies and outcomes. Emphasize self-sufficiency and long-term partnership. Scale through systems and automation, not headcount.

**Brand Donts:**
Don't use long lists of services or capabilities. Don't speak in corporate jargon or algorithmic buzzwords. Don't oversell what AI can do or present it as a silver bullet. Don't promise things you can't deliver. Don't hide behind formal language when candor would be clearer.

Don't use long lists of trendy services. Don't oversell capabilities or use corporate jargon like 'algorithmic vibe alignment.' Don't promise quick fixes without understanding the real problem. Don't abandon clients after the initial engagement.
