**Overview:**
Wrinkles Schminkles specializes in custom-designed, clinically proven silicone patches that target areas of the skin most prone to wrinkles and fine lines. Their signature products are reusable silicone patches designed to improve the appearance of unwanted lines and wrinkles in a matter of hours, with visible skin refinement from regular use. The brand positions itself as a judgement-free zone for consumers seeking non-invasive alternatives to procedures like Botox. Their product range includes patches targeting specific areas: forehead, eyes, mouth, chest, neck, and body. The company has gained significant viral attention on social media platforms like TikTok for their before-and-after results, positioning their products as affordable alternatives to in-office cosmetic procedures. They also offer complementary products including microneedle patches, sheet masks, and peel pads.

Wrinkles Schminkles is a skincare brand founded by Gabrielle Requena that specializes in medical-grade silicone patches designed to refine lines and wrinkles. The company creates reusable silicone patches for the face, chest, hands, and neck that are transparent, safe for all skin types, and designed to visibly smooth lines and wrinkles in a matter of hours. The patches are developed to be a non-invasive alternative to expensive professional procedures or complex skincare routines, offering targeted treatment for unwanted lines caused by sun damage, side-sleeping, and the ageing process. The silicone patches are easy to incorporate into normal skincare routines and can be worn for a few hours or overnight. The products have undergone extensive clinical testing to prove their efficacy in visibly smoothing lines and wrinkles while strengthening skin over time.

## Purpose & Values

**Brand Purpose:**
To provide a non-invasive, sophisticated skincare solution using Medical Grade Silicone to help improve the appearance of unwanted lines and wrinkles caused by sun damage, side-sleeping and the ageing process.

**Core Values:**
- **Accessibility**: Making effective skincare solutions available to everyone without requiring expensive professional procedures or complicated routines
- **Safety**: Medical Grade Silicone that is safe for all skin types and commonly used by doctors and dermatologists
- **Innovation**: Pioneer of Skincare That Sticks - creating sophisticated, targeted use of silicone technology refined through extensive testing
- **Sustainability**: Commitment to sustainability across patches, people, and the planet
- **Acceptance**: Respecting that ageing is a natural part of growing older while providing options for those who want to address visible signs of aging

**Value Proposition:**
Reusable Silicone Patches that visibly smooth lines and wrinkles in a matter of hours, strengthen skin over time, and are easy to integrate into normal skincare routines without requiring 12-step routines or expensive professional procedures.

## Brand Guidelines

**Tone of Voice:**
Honest, relatable, and empowering. The brand acknowledges the complexity and conflicting feelings around aging while offering a practical, non-judgmental solution. The tone is conversational and understanding, avoiding shame while celebrating choice and self-acceptance.

**Key Words:** relatable, honest, empowering, non-judgmental, sophisticated

**Typography:** Primary: Albra_med | Secondary: Roboto, sans-serif | Albra_med used for headings (h1, h2) at weights 500. Roboto used for body text, links, and buttons at weight 400.

**Colour Palette:**
- Primary Blue (#0f1099) - Primary brand color - used for headings, links, borders, text
- White (#ffffff) - Background and button text

**Brand Dos:**
Celebrate the complexity of aging. Offer non-judgmental solutions. Emphasize clinical proof and safety. Focus on ease of use and integration into existing routines. Highlight the medical-grade quality and dermatologist-backed science.

Celebrate the complexity of aging choices. Be honest about conflicting feelings around appearance and aging. Position the product as one option among many valid approaches to skincare. Use before and after imagery and clinical evidence to demonstrate results. Emphasize the non-invasive, practical nature of the solution.

**Brand Donts:**
Shame consumers for wanting to address aging or for accepting natural changes. Promote invasive or extreme procedures. Oversimplify the emotional complexity of aging and appearance.

Don't shame people for wanting to address wrinkles or for accepting natural aging. Don't position the product as the only solution or imply judgment about other skincare approaches. Don't make exaggerated claims beyond what clinical testing has proven.
