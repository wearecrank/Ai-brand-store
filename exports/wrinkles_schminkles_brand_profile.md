# Organisation Profile: Wrinkles Schminkles

## Organisation Info

**Brand Name:** Wrinkles Schminkles

**Tagline:** Real Wrinkles Unreal Results

**Industry:** Beauty & Skincare

**Overview:**
Wrinkles Schminkles specializes in custom-designed silicone patches clinically proven to target areas of skin most prone to wrinkles and fine lines. Their signature products include silicone patches, microneedle patches, sheet masks, and peel pads designed to improve the appearance of unwanted lines and wrinkles in various facial areas including the forehead, eyes, mouth, chest, neck, and décolletage. The company positions itself as a judgment-free zone for anti-aging skincare, offering an affordable alternative to professional treatments like Botox. Their products are reusable and aim to deliver real skin refinement with regular use. The brand has gained significant viral attention on social media platforms, particularly TikTok, for their before and after results.
