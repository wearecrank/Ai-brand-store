# Organisation Profile: Vieweet

## Organisation Info

**Brand Name:** Vieweet

**Tagline:** One Capture. All Your Data Deliverables.

**Industry:** Property Data & AI Technology

**Overview:**
Vieweet is a visual data company using in-house AI models trained on over 3 million proprietary 360° images. The company digitally surveys property images and delivers cost-effective, accurate, and actionable data in clear, structured reports. Vieweet turns a single 360° capture into rich, high-value property data including 3D models, floor plans, room measurements, ceiling heights, and bespoke reports. The platform serves multiple industries including energy assessment, property valuation, property management, insurance claims, and sales & lettings. Key features include 3D digital twins, built-in measurement tools, heat loss transfer zones, API-ready data export, and a property database dashboard. Vieweet offers significant advantages over traditional methods: 3x faster than LiDAR (completing a 3-bed property in 25-30 minutes vs 60+ minutes), 90% lower cost (hardware from £300 vs £2k-£3k+), and 98% measurement accuracy. The company has captured over 22 million square meters of properties and partners with industry leaders to deliver integrated solutions for property professionals.
